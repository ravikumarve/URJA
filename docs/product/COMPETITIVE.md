## Key Gaps in the Market

1. **No renewable energy boilerplate exists** — The boilerplate market is entirely generic (auth, payments, DB). Zero domain-specific options for energy.
2. **Enterprise options are SaaS-only** — Power Factors, Fluence, Wärtsilä are all proprietary cloud services. You can't buy, customize, or resell their code.
3. **Open-source tools are research-grade** — PyPSA, NREL tools are libraries for simulation, not operational platforms.
4. **Custom development is expensive** — A basic solar monitoring dashboard costs $50K-$200K to build from scratch.
